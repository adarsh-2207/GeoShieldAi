# References and Data Sources

The project methodology was informed by published work on Jharia coalfield fires, subsidence and remote sensing.

## Research / background

1. Das, A. (2017). *Jharia Coal Mine Fire and its Impact*. Jharkhand Journal of Development and Management Studies, XISS Ranchi.
2. Chatterjee, R. S., Lakhera, R. C., & Dadhwal, V. K. (2010). *InSAR coherence and phase information for mapping environmental indicators of opencast coal mining: a case study in Jharia Coalfield*. Canadian Journal of Remote Sensing, 36(4), 361–373.
3. ISRO / NRSC, *Space Science Exploration and Research in India*, 2020–21. Material concerning Jharia coalfield fire and land-subsidence monitoring.
4. Bharat Coking Coal Limited (BCCL), *Master Plan for Dealing with Fire, Subsidence, and Rehabilitation in the Leasehold of BCCL*, 2008.
5. Geological Survey of India, *Geological Report on Lohapatti East Production Support*, 2013.
